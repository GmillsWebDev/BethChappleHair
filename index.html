About Me

Hi, I’m Beth!
Your Local Specialist for Natural Colour, Fine & Curly Hair in Petersfield.

With over 17 years of experience in the hair industry, I recently decided to start my own adventure right here in Petersfield. My career has taken me on some incredible journeys—from styling hair on luxury cruise ships while travelling the world to working behind the scenes at Milan Fashion Week.

While the catwalks were exciting, my heart has always been at home, helping my lovely local clients feel their best. I specialise in fine hair, curly hair, and natural colour work, ranging from low-maintenance "lived-in" looks to seamless grey blending.

For me, it’s about more than just a trim. I love the "magic" of a transformation—how a fresh set of highlights or a simple root touch-up can completely lift your mood. Whether you need a good heart-to-heart or just want to walk out feeling like a new person, I’m here to make sure you leave the chair looking and feeling incredible.

Quality Products

I only work with high quality products from companies like Wella, Moroccanoil and Milkshake. I love working with Wella colours, I believe that with a good colour, no matter how small or big, we can always create some magic.

A Little About Me

When I’m not in the salon, I’m a busy mum of two with a very spoiled "furbaby", so I’m always up for a chat about kids or dogs!

Ready for a chat?

I’d love to meet you. Please get in touch to book a free colour consultation or just to have a chat about your hair goals.

HBA Awards

It was an incredible honour to represent Petersfield as a finalist in the 2024 Hair and Beauty Awards (HBA). Being recognised on a national level was a huge milestone in my career and a wonderful reminder of why I love what I do. I am so lucky to have such a supportive community behind me; my amazing clients have cheered me on since day one. Thank you, Petersfield, for making this town feel like home and for being part of this journey with me.

You can read more in the Farnham Herald.

In 2026 I was proud to be placed 43rd in the UK for Hair Stylist of the Year.
