Salon Policies

Last updated: 12/08/2023

Bookings

Any new guests booking a colour appointment worth over £100 will be required to pay a 50% deposit at the time of booking.

All new colour guests will be required to have a skin test and a consultation at least 48 hours prior to any service.

Bookings can be made via Phone, Email, Text, Facebook, Instagram, or the contact section of this website.

A booking involving a colouring service must also include a cut and finish (or blowdry).

Cancellations

I appreciate that from time to time, life gets in the way and things need to be rearranged. However, for any appointment cancelled or rescheduled:

  • Within 48 hours of their booked appointment, there will be a 50% charge of the total services.
  • Within 24 hours of their booked appointment, there will be a 100% charge of the total services.

Any no show appointments will be charged at 100% of the services booked. If this occurs twice then any future bookings will require a 100% non-refundable deposit at the time of booking.

Illness

Due to having a very young child I please ask that if you are ill or display symptoms of COVID-19 prior to your appointment, please can you contact me as we may need to rearrange your appointment. I have to protect myself and my family so appreciate your understanding.
