Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16/11/2026

What are cookies?

A cookie is a small piece of information that a website stores on your device. Cookies let a website remember things between visits, and some are used to measure how a site is used.

How this website uses cookies

This website only uses optional cookies if you agree to them. When you first visit, a banner asks for your choice, and you can change it at any time using the settings below.

Strictly necessary

Your cookie choice is remembered in your browser's local storage (under the name bch-consent) so you are not asked on every page. This is needed to respect your choice, cannot be switched off, and contains no personal information.

Analytics (optional)

If you allow it, Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga and _ga_*, which last up to 2 years) to count visits and show which pages are popular. This helps me improve the website but does not tell me who you are. The data is processed by Google; see Google's privacy policy.

Google Maps (optional)

The map on the home page is provided by Google, which may set its own cookies when the map loads. The map only loads if you allow it.

Your cookie settings

Turning analytics off also removes the analytics cookies this website has set.

Blocking or deleting cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies using your browser settings. Here are links for the most popular browsers:

  • Chrome
  • Edge
  • Firefox
  • Safari

Where can I get more information?

All About Cookies and the Information Commissioner's Office both have helpful guides.
